Xiao Ning

I am a Medical Data Scientist at the Heidelberg Institute of Global Health (HIGH), Heidelberg University, Germany. My research lies at the intersection of artificial intelligence, statistical learning, and computational modelling, with applications in ecology, climate, and global health.

I am interested in developing reliable AI and statistical methods for scientific discovery, particularly in uncertainty quantification, robust machine learning, and the integration of data-driven and mechanistic models.

Research Interests

  • AI for Science — Scientific machine learning, deep learning, physics-informed AI
  • Statistical Learning — Bayesian inference, uncertainty quantification, domain generalization
  • Computational Modelling — Stochastic differential equations, dynamical systems, data assimilation
  • Computational Ecology — Bioacoustics, ecological monitoring, biodiversity inference
  • Climate & Global Health — Climate-sensitive infectious diseases, environmental health modelling
