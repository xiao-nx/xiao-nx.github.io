Welcome! I'm Xiao Ning, a researcher at Heidelberg University, Germany, working at the intersection of artificial intelligence, statistical learning, and environmental health.

My research focuses on developing AI-driven and statistical methods for understanding complex ecological and epidemiological systems. I am particularly interested in integrating machine learning, probabilistic inference, and mathematical modelling with environmental and biological data to improve ecological monitoring, quantify uncertainty, and understand the impacts of environmental change on health.

My current work spans AI-enabled bioacoustic monitoring, statistical ecological inference, climate-sensitive infectious disease modelling, and trustworthy machine learning. Through interdisciplinary research, I aim to bridge methodological advances with real-world applications in environmental surveillance and public health.

I welcome interdisciplinary research collaborations, industry partnerships, and student research projects.

Research Interests
AI for Environmental Monitoring: Bioacoustic sensing, automated species recognition, and ecological monitoring.

Statistical Learning & Uncertainty Quantification: Probabilistic modelling, Bayesian inference, and robust machine learning.

Ecological & Epidemiological Modelling: Mathematical models, spatiotemporal dynamics, and data assimilation.

Climate Change & Health: Environmental drivers of vector ecology, infectious disease dynamics, and climate-related health risks.
