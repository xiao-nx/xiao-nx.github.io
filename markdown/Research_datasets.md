

### Mosquito Bioacoustic Monitoring Dataset

My research involves the collection, curation, and analysis of scientific datasets, with a particular focus on **bioacoustics, ecological monitoring, and climate-sensitive infectious diseases**.

**Research Area:** Bioacoustics · Computational Ecology · AI for Science

**Status:** In development

Aims to develop standardized mosquito bioacoustic datasets for machine learning and ecological inference.
The dataset development focuses on acoustic recordings, environmental conditions, recording devices, and biological metadata to support:

- Mosquito acoustic detection and species classification
- Cross-domain generalization and model robustness
- Temperature-dependent acoustic variation
- Behavioural and ecological inference
- Uncertainty-aware acoustic monitoring

**Data availability:** Not yet publicly released.

### Public Data Resources

The following publicly available datasets support research in mosquito bioacoustics, acoustic species classification, and ecological monitoring. Please refer to the original publications and repositories for data access, licensing, and citation information.

| Dataset | Year | Description | Resources |
|:---|:---:|:---|:---|
| Wingbeats | 2014 | Mosquito wingbeat recordings for acoustic species classification. | [UCR Archive](https://www.timeseriesclassification.com/) |
| Mosquito Acoustic Dataset (eLife) | 2017 | Mosquito flight sound recordings for acoustic detection and species identification. | [Dryad](https://datadryad.org/) |
| Mosquito Acoustic Dataset (Scientific Data) | 2020 | Mosquito acoustic recordings for machine learning and species recognition. | [Scientific Data](https://www.nature.com/sdata/) |
| HumBug Zooniverse | 2020 | Citizen-science annotations of mosquito acoustic recordings. | [HumBug](https://humbug.ac.uk/) |
| HumBugDB | 2021 | Mosquito acoustic recordings with species and recording metadata. | [HumBug](https://humbug.ac.uk/) |
| MosquitoSong+ | 2024 | Mosquito acoustic data for species classification and machine learning. | — |
| Environmental Wingbeat Dataset | 2025 | Mosquito wingbeat frequency measurements with biological and environmental variables. | [Figshare](https://figshare.com/) |
| BioDCASE CD-MSC | 2026 | Cross-domain mosquito species classification benchmark with heterogeneous recording conditions. | — |
