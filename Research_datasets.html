Mosquito Bioacoustic Monitoring Dataset

My research involves the collection, curation, and analysis of scientific datasets, with a particular focus on bioacoustics, ecological monitoring, and climate-sensitive infectious diseases.

Research Area: Bioacoustics · Computational Ecology · AI for Science

Status: In development

Aims to develop standardized mosquito bioacoustic datasets for machine learning and ecological inference. The dataset development focuses on acoustic recordings, environmental conditions, recording devices, and biological metadata to support:

  • Mosquito acoustic detection and species classification
  • Cross-domain generalization and model robustness
  • Temperature-dependent acoustic variation
  • Behavioural and ecological inference
  • Uncertainty-aware acoustic monitoring

Data availability: Not yet publicly released.

Public Data Resources

The following publicly available datasets support research in mosquito bioacoustics, acoustic species classification, and ecological monitoring. Please refer to the original publications and repositories for data access, licensing, and citation information.

Dataset Year Description Resources
