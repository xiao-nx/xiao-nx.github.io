Opportunities

I welcome motivated students and researchers interested in AI for Science, statistical learning, and computational modelling, with applications in ecology and global health.

Student Opportunities

I am happy to discuss potential student projects, including:

  • 🎓 Master's Theses — Research-oriented projects involving AI, statistical methods, and computational modelling.
  • 💻 Research Practicals & Internships — Hands-on projects in machine learning, scientific programming, and data analysis.

Potential Research Topics

  • Trustworthy AI & Statistical Learning
  • Scientific Machine Learning & Computational Modelling
  • Bioacoustics & Ecological Monitoring
  • Climate & Global Health

Research Collaboration

I welcome interdisciplinary collaborations involving AI, statistics, computational modelling, ecology, and global health, particularly joint methodological development and scientific applications.
